Week 6 Assignment — Build an Economic Dashboard

Week 6 Assignment — Build an Economic Dashboard

Item Details
Week Week 6
Topic Interactive Dashboards and Data Storytelling
Marks 5 Marks
Duration 1 Hour
Submission Completed Google Colab notebook link

Purpose

This assignment checks whether you can communicate economic evidence clearly using an interactive dashboard.

Your dashboard should help a reader understand the main macroeconomic pattern in the dataset.

This assignment builds directly from Week 5. You should use the evidence and interpretation developed in Week 5 as the foundation for your dashboard story.

Materials

Material Link
Week 6 Live Notebook Open in Google Colab
Dataset Download sri_lanka_macro.csv
Plotly Guide Open Plotly Python Guide

Before working:

File -> Save a copy in Drive

Rename your notebook:

Week06_IndexNumber_Name.ipynb

Task 1 — Dashboard Question

Write one dashboard question.

Example:

How did Sri Lanka's macroeconomic conditions change across the period?

Then identify the three indicators your dashboard will use.

Marks: 1

Expected output:

  • one clear dashboard question
  • three selected indicators
  • short explanation of why those indicators matter

Task 2 — Interactive Charts

Create three interactive Plotly charts:

Chart Required Variable
Line chart Inflation over time
Bar chart GDP growth by year
Scatter plot Inflation and exchange rate change

Each chart must include:

  • meaningful title
  • clear variable names
  • hover information where useful

Marks: 1

Expected output:

  • three working Plotly charts
  • meaningful titles
  • readable labels
  • hover information where useful
  • chart choices linked to the dashboard question

Task 3 — Multi-Panel Dashboard

Create one multi-panel dashboard using Plotly subplots.

Your dashboard should include at least three indicators.

Suggested structure:

Panel Indicator
Top Inflation
Middle GDP growth
Bottom Exchange rate or reserves

Marks: 1

Expected output:

  • one multi-panel dashboard
  • at least three indicators
  • coherent ordering of panels
  • readable layout

Task 4 — Dashboard Design Review

Write 80-120 words evaluating your dashboard design.

Mention:

  • what the reader should notice first
  • why you selected these charts
  • one design improvement you made
  • one limitation of the dashboard

Marks: 1

Expected output:

  • 80-120 word design review
  • explanation of what the reader should notice first
  • justification for chart choices
  • one design improvement
  • one limitation

Task 5 — Data Story

Write a final dashboard story of 120-180 words.

Your story must include:

  • main claim
  • evidence from at least two indicators
  • economic meaning
  • caution about interpretation

Marks: 1

Expected output:

  • 120-180 word dashboard story
  • clear claim
  • evidence from at least two indicators
  • economic meaning
  • caution about interpretation

Marking Rubric

Criterion Mark
Dashboard question and indicators are clear 1
Interactive charts are complete and purposeful 1
Multi-panel dashboard is coherent and readable 1
Design review is thoughtful and specific 1
Dashboard story is evidence-based and cautious 1

Submission Checklist

Before submitting, make sure:

  • all charts display correctly
  • the dashboard has a clear title
  • chart labels are readable
  • interpretation is written in your own words
  • sharing is set to Anyone with the link -> Viewer

The lecturer will provide the live submission link during the class session.
