ECN 4199 — Weekly In-Class Activities

This page contains the weekly in-class activities for ECN 4199: Industrial Data Analytics.

Each activity must be completed during the scheduled lecture hour.

Assessment Structure

Item Details
Number of Activities 13
Marks per Activity 5 marks
Total Marks 65 marks
Duration 1 hour per activity
Access Lecturer passcode required
Submission Through weekly submission link
Completion Time During lecture only

Important Instructions

Students must:

  1. Attend the scheduled lecture.
  2. Wait until the lecturer announces the passcode.
  3. Open the relevant weekly activity.
  4. Complete the activity within one hour.
  5. Submit before the activity closes.

Late submissions will not be accepted unless approved by the lecturer.

How Activities Work

Each weekly activity may include:

  • short conceptual questions
  • Python coding tasks
  • interpretation questions
  • graph or output interpretation
  • short reflection questions

The purpose is not memorisation.

The purpose is to test whether students can apply the weekly concepts in real time.

Passcode Access

Each activity will be protected by a lecturer-set passcode.

The passcode will be announced only during the relevant lecture.

Students should not share passcodes with others.

Submission Format

Each activity must be submitted through the relevant weekly activity link.

Where a Colab notebook is required, students must:

  1. Save a copy in Google Drive.
  2. Rename the notebook properly.
  3. Set sharing to:
Anyone with the link → Viewer
  1. Submit the notebook link.

Notebook Naming Convention

Use the following format:

WeekNumber_IndexNumber_Name.ipynb

Example:

Week05_2024BA12345_Perera.ipynb

Marking

Each weekly activity is worth 5 marks.

The total mark across all 13 activities is:

13 × 5 = 65 marks

Marks will be based on:

Component Marks
Correct completion of required task 2
Correct code/output 1
Economic interpretation 1
Timely submission and formatting 1
Total 5

Academic Integrity

Students may use course materials and lecture notebooks.

However:

  • copying another student’s work is not allowed
  • submitting work without understanding is not allowed
  • AI tools must not be used to replace student reasoning
  • all submitted explanations must be written in the student’s own words

Lecturer Note

The weekly links will be activated or shared during the relevant lecture period only.
